# Privacy Policy — VELOCITY

**Last Updated: November 2026**

## Core Privacy Commitment
VELOCITY is designed from the ground up to respect your fundamental right to privacy:

1. **Zero Mandatory Telemetry**: VELOCITY collects NO telemetry, NO analytics, NO tracking beacons, and NO user activity identifiers.
2. **Zero Cloud Dependencies**: The application does not require user accounts, email registration, phone numbers, or subscriptions. All settings and download histories remain stored strictly on your local computer.
3. **Zero Third-Party Advertising**: VELOCITY contains no ads, sponsors, or bundled monetization toolbars.
4. **Local Proxy & VPN Credentials**: Any proxy passwords configured by the user are encrypted locally using the Windows Data Protection API (`CryptProtectData`) and are never transmitted over the internet.
5. **BitTorrent Protocol Transparency**: When downloading via public BitTorrent swarms, your public IP address is shared with other peers in that swarm as mandated by the decentralized BitTorrent protocol specification. To shield your IP from remote peers, use a reputable VPN service combined with VELOCITY's **Network Lock** feature.
